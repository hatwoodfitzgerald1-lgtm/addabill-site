    Adding a bill

    Add a bill in the time it takes to open the envelope

    Type the biller, the due day and the amount if you know it, or point the camera at the paper. Addabill reads the amount and the due date, sets the bill on its day, and stamps it Added. It's quicker than finding the envelope was.

    The paper bills stop hiding

    The dentist's statement, the lawn service, the county's yearly notice. Photograph the paper and it stands on the same month as the electric, so nothing is off the calendar just because it came through the mailbox.

    The heads-up reaches whoever pays it

    On Household the calendar is shared with up to four other people and shows who handles each bill, and each person's texts go to their own phone. You stop being the house's reminder.

    Pay it where you always have

    The link in each text opens that biller's own payment page. Same login you already use, same card you already pay from, and not a cent added by us.

    NeverWhy
    Never pays a bill for youso money moves only when you move it, from the page you already use.
